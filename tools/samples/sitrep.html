1. Event

An overnight storm cell with wind gusts above 100 km/h brought down an aerial fibre span (48-core trunk) between poles KTH-114 and KTH-117. Two poles are leaning; the cable is on the road verge. Alarms raised 02:15 ACST.

2. Impact

  • 2 mobile sites offline (KTH-South, KTH-Highway), on battery, about 4 h left.
  • LoRaWAN gateway GW-KTH-03 offline: it carries the regional clinic's vaccine cold-chain and patient monitoring sensors.
  • About 1,200 customers reported affected (mobile and fixed wireless). Emergency calls rerouting through neighbouring cells with reduced coverage.
